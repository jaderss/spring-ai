# Frequently asked questions

Acme Bank is a fictional bank. These answers exist only for this demo.

## Is Acme Bank a real bank

No. Acme Bank, its customers, accounts and merchants are fictional and exist to demonstrate the assistant.

## How do I confirm a transfer

The assistant only proposes transfers. Every proposal appears in the app, where the customer confirms it. A proposal
expires ten minutes after it is created, after which the customer needs to ask for a new one.

## Can the assistant give financial advice

No. The assistant answers questions about the customer's own accounts, transactions, transfers and the bank's published
fees and terms. It does not give investment, tax or credit advice.

## How do I report a lost card

Call the fictional card hotline at 555-0100 any time. The assistant cannot block cards.
