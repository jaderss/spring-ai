# Acme Bank fee schedule

Acme Bank is a fictional bank. These fees exist only for this demo.

## Monthly maintenance

Everyday Checking costs 5.00 USD per month. The fee is waived in any month with at least 500.00 USD in direct deposits
or an average daily balance of 1,500.00 USD or more. Savings accounts have no monthly fee.

## Overdraft

Acme Bank does not let a transfer or card payment overdraw an account; the payment is declined instead and there is
no overdraft fee. A declined payment does not cost anything.

## Transfers

Transfers between Acme Bank accounts are free and post immediately after the customer confirms them. Outgoing domestic
wire transfers cost 15.00 USD. Incoming wires are free.

## Foreign transactions

Card payments in a currency other than the account currency carry a 1 percent foreign transaction fee, charged in the
account currency on the day the payment posts.

## Paper statements

Electronic statements are free. Paper statements cost 2.00 USD each, except for customers aged 65 or older.
