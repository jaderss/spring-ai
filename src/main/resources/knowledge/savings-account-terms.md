# High Yield Savings terms

Acme Bank is a fictional bank. These terms exist only for this demo.

## Interest rate

High Yield Savings earns 1.50 percent annual percentage yield. Interest accrues daily and is paid on the last day of
each month. The rate is variable and may change at any time.

## Withdrawals

Customers may move money out of savings by transfer at any time. There is no limit on the number of withdrawals and no
fee for them.

## Minimum balance

There is no minimum balance. Interest is paid on any positive balance.
